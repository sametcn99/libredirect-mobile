LibRedirect Mobile redirects links from big platforms (YouTube, Reddit, X/Twitter and more) to privacy-friendly frontends such as Invidious, Piped or Redlib — automatically, entirely on your device, with no accounts and no telemetry.

ROUTING
• System-wide: set it as your default browser or share any link to it.
• Open routed links in the built-in multi-tab browser or your preferred external browser.
• Per-service control: always route, ask every time, or switch a service off.
• Automatic instance selection weighted by measured latency, with automatic failover when an instance is down.
• Routing exceptions: domains, path prefixes, wildcards, and per-app exclusions.
• Temporarily pause routing for 15 minutes, an hour, or until you turn it back on.
• "Why?" explainer shows exactly what happened to any link you test.

PRIVACY
• Tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, and more) are stripped locally, including host-specific ones you define yourself.
• Redirect wrappers (google.com/url, out.reddit, AMP caches…) are unwrapped entirely offline.
• Optional (off by default) short-link resolution, strictly limited to a fixed allowlist.
• Local content blocking of well-known tracker hosts inside the built-in browser.
• A local privacy statistics card — counters stay on your device.

BUILT-IN BROWSER
• Multi-tab browsing with incognito tabs (ephemeral profile), bookmarks, history with configurable limits.
• Picture-in-picture video, background audio playback (opt-in), reader mode, print to PDF, per-site settings.
• Built-in download manager (pause/resume, Wi-Fi-only option, pre-download rename and safety warnings, smart media/proxy filename resolution, category filters and search), find in page, pull-to-refresh.

EVERYTHING IS LOCAL
• No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts. Routing data is generated on-device from the live LibRedirect upstream lists and validated before activation. Fail-open: if routing cannot decide, the original link opens unchanged.
